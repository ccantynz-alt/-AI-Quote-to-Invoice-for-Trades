AI-powered quoting and invoicing for trades and local service businesses. Generate professional quotes in 2 minutes.

== Description ==

TradeQuote AI helps plumbers, electricians, builders, painters, landscapers, and any local service business create professional quotes and invoices directly from their WordPress dashboard — powered by AI.

Stop spending an hour on every quote. Describe the job in plain English, let AI build the line items, review, and send. That's it.

**Features:**

* AI quote generation from plain English job descriptions
* One-click quote-to-invoice conversion
* Professional branded PDF quotes and invoices
* Email delivery direct from WordPress
* Customer database with full job history
* Job tracker dashboard (Draft → Sent → Accepted → Invoiced → Paid)
* Bring your own Claude API key (you control your AI costs)
* NZ GST, AU GST, and custom tax rate support

**How it works:**

1. Install and activate the plugin
2. Go to TradeQuote AI → Settings and enter your business details + Claude API key
3. Click "New Quote", describe the job, and let AI generate your line items
4. Review, adjust if needed, then save or send directly to your customer

**Pricing:**

* **Free** — 5 quotes/month, includes AI builder, PDF, email, customer DB
* **Pro** ($29/month) — Unlimited quotes, invoice conversion, job tracker, custom branding
* **Business** ($59/month) — Everything Pro + multi-user, priority support

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `tradequote-ai` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`
2. Activate through the Plugins menu
3. Go to TradeQuote AI → Settings
4. Enter your business details and Claude API key from [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com/)

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need a Claude API key? =

Yes. TradeQuote AI uses a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model so you control your AI costs directly. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

= Is my data stored securely? =

All data is stored in your own WordPress database. Your API key is stored encrypted in WordPress options.

= What countries does it support? =

Tax rates are fully configurable — set 15% for NZ GST, 10% for AU GST, 20% for UK VAT, or any custom rate.

= Can I customise the PDF branding? =

Yes. Upload your logo, set your business name, tax number, and payment terms in Settings.
